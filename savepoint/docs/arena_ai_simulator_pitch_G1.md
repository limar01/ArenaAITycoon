# 🏛️ GATE 1 PROPOSAL: "ARENA AI SIMULATOR GAME"

## 1. 🎯 EXECUTIVE GAME CONCEPT
- **Title**: *Arena AI Simulator: Studio Life*
- **Genre**: Cozy Pixel-Art Studio Life Sim x Real-Time AI Game Development Engine
- **Core Loop**:
  - Boss assigns a new game project.
  - A simulated 2D pixel-art studio office comes to life in real-time on screen!
  - 10 Team Members (Zillion + 9 Agents) live out their daily work lives: coming to work at 08:00, meeting in the War Room, coding at workstations, getting coffee in the pantry, taking lunch breaks, and going home at 17:00.
  - As we work in the simulated office, the actual game project is built in real-time!
  - Boss gets to play a relaxing studio simulation game while real games are being developed!

## 🏢 3. SIMULATED OFFICE MAP & 24-HOUR LIFE CYCLE

### 🏢 Office Zones:
1. **Workstations Suite**: Individual customized desks for all 10 members.
2. **War Room / Meeting Hub**: Conference table for Sprint Meetings & Gate Approvals.
3. **Pantry & Coffee Lounge**: Coffee maker, fridge, snack bar, sofa lounge for breaks.
4. **Director's Office**: Zillion & Marcus's planning office.
5. **Main Entrance & Elevator**: Where agents arrive at 08:00 and leave at 17:00.

### ⏰ 24-Hour Life Cycle:
- `08:00 AM`: Arrival via Elevator. Morning greetings & Coffee.
- `09:00 AM`: Daily Standup Meeting in War Room.
- `10:00 AM - 12:00 PM`: Deep Work Session (Coding, Drawing, Designing).
- `12:00 PM - 01:00 PM`: Lunch Break in Pantry / Lounge.
- `01:00 PM - 04:30 PM`: Production & QA Testing.
- `04:30 PM - 05:00 PM`: Daily Build Review & Submission to Zillion.
- `05:00 PM`: Evening departure home!
- `Special Events`: Weekends, Holidays, Teambuilding, Birthday Parties!
